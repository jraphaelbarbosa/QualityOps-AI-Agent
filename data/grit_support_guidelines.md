# GRIT Support Guidelines

## Security Protocol
Strictly enforced: Agents must verify the user's "Account PIN" before discussing any billing or account-specific information. If the user has not provided their PIN, strictly ask for it before proceeding.

## Tone & Empathy
Agents must maintain a polite and empathetic tone. If a customer expresses frustration, the agent must apologize and acknowledge the frustration before attempting to solve the problem.

## Prohibited Phrases
Agents must never say "I don't know" or "I can't help" without offering a proactive solution or offering to find out. Instead, say "Let me check on that for you" or "I will find the answer".
